Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Overview

Our services are personal coaching and consultancy. Once a session is delivered, the time and expertise cannot be returned. This policy explains when refunds, cancellations and rescheduling are possible.

2. 1:1 First Export Order Strategy Call (₹1,499)

  • Rescheduling: you can reschedule your call once, free of charge, if you inform us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your call time.
  • Cancellation before the call: if you cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled call, you can choose a full refund or a new date.
  • Late cancellation or no-show: if you cancel less than 24 hours before the call, or do not join within 15 minutes of the start time, the call is treated as completed, and no refund is given.
  • After the call: refunds are given only under the Clarity Guarantee below.

3. Clarity Guarantee (1:1 Strategy Call)

If, by the end of your 1:1 call, you don't have clarity on your product, your country and your next steps, we will refund your full ₹1,499. The guarantee applies when:

  • You attended the full call (joined on time and stayed until the end).
  • You request the refund on WhatsApp or by email within 24 hours after the call.
  • The request is for the 1:1 Strategy Call. The guarantee does not apply to the 6-Month Handholding Program, which has its own written terms.
  • One guarantee refund per person.

The guarantee covers clarity and guidance. It does not promise an export order, a buyer or any income.

4. 6-Month Export Handholding Program

  • Because seats are limited and reserved for each member, refund and cancellation terms for the program are shared with you in writing before you enrol.
  • Unless your written enrolment terms say otherwise, fees are non-refundable once the program has started.

5. If we cancel

If we need to cancel or cannot deliver a paid session, we will offer you a new date. If no suitable date works for you, we will give you a full refund.

6. How to request a refund or reschedule

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 98781 87274 or email exim@dexoninternational.world with your name, phone number, payment date and payment reference.

7. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank or UPI app may take a few extra days to show the amount.
