Frequently Asked Questions

I am completely new to export. Is this for me?

Yes. This call is made for people who want their first export order. Many of our clients came to the call with no product finalised.

Will you give me a buyer list?

No. A list alone rarely brings a first order. On the call, you learn how to find and approach buyers yourself, so you don't depend on anyone.

I don't have an IEC or a registered company yet. Can I still book?

Yes. Many new exporters start here. On the call, we tell you which documents you need, like IEC and GST, and in what order, so you don't waste time or money.

I don't have my own factory or product. Can I still export?

Yes. Many exporters start by sourcing from a good manufacturer. On the call, we help you choose the right product and explain how to pick a reliable supplier.

How much money do I need to start exporting?

It depends on your product and country. On the call, we look at your budget and choose a product and market that fit it, so you start small and safe.

I already bought a course or buyer data, but got no orders. How is this different?

A course is the same for everyone, and a list doesn't tell you how to talk to buyers. This call is only about your product, your country and your questions, and you leave with a step-by-step plan.

Will I get an export order right after the call?

Honestly, no one can promise an order. What you get is a clear plan: what to sell, where to sell, how to find buyers and what to say to them. The results come from following that plan, step by step.
